Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet & Pro Tips Keep Open on a Second Screen
10 Golden Rules for a High-Scoring Demo
  • 1. Deliberate cursor movements: Don't jerk the mouse. Hover slowly and point precisely at what you're discussing.
  • 2. Pause after modals open: Wait 1 second so the viewer sees the form before you start typing.
  • 3. Explain the WHY: Don't just say "I'll click this". Explain why row locks matter and why prices are snapshotted.
  • 4. Use the 1-click login buttons: Shows developer ergonomics and saves time on camera.
  • 5. Show cause and effect: After confirming a challan, switch to Products to show stock went down.
  • 6. Highlight safety checks: Point at low-stock badges, overdue follow-ups, and the delete confirmation modal.
  • 7. Do one dry run: Practice the full flow once without recording. Builds confidence and finds data gaps.
  • 8. Speak clearly: Moderate pace, confident tone, avoid filler words ("um", "like").
  • 9. Record in 1080p 60fps: Use OBS Studio (free) or Windows Snipping Tool (Win+Shift+R).
  • 10. Mention the tech stack naturally: Weave in React 19, TypeScript, Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL Neon.

Emergency Troubleshooting (If Something Goes Wrong) Quick Fixes
What Went Wrong Why It Happened What to Do Right Now
Login fails / 401 error Stale token in browser storage Press F5 to refresh, then click the 1-click Admin button again.
"Adjust Stock" is grayed out You're logged in as Sales or Accounts Sign Out → login as Admin or Warehouse.
